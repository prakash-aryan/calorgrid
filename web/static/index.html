Live: the scheduler drives a real job on this laptop

The same HeatShift.allocate() as the 8,760-hour simulation runs once per simulated hour. When the house has no use for heat, the real process is frozen (SIGSTOP). When heat is needed again, it continues (SIGCONT). Migration sends SIGTERM: the job saves a checkpoint, then resumes on another server.
