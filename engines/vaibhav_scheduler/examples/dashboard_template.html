Workload and prices

Set the share of arriving jobs in each tier, then run. Every tier's GPU-hour price is the base price P, the spot price, times its factor. Prices can be changed while the run goes on; they do not change where jobs go.

Costs and backup heating

Backup heating is what the server heat replaces: the real house heats with gas, so a gas boiler is the default; switch to a heat pump to compare. Of the percentages, only maintenance has a value to start with, and it is a guess. The others are costs a cloud provider's price has to cover; they stay out of the result until you enter them. Like prices, all of these can be changed while the run goes on.
