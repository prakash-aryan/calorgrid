Costs, income and heat savings

Servers in the house

Each server is a system whose power was measured in MLPerf Inference. Its jobs are real tasks from the Alibaba GPU cluster trace. A server draws its measured full-load power times the share of its GPUs that are busy, and 80% of that electricity is recovered as heat.

Money for this day

Everything is for the one day shown. Hardware and maintenance are a fixed amount per server per day, whether or not the server is busy. The heat savings are the heating bill the house no longer pays: backup heat with the servers compared with the same day without them.

Heat produced by the servers and absorbed by the house, kWh in each hour

A column is the most heat the house can take in that hour. The solid part is server heat it absorbs; the pale part is room still left. The tick is the least the house must get: where the solid part stops below it, the backup heater fills the gap. Server heat beyond the column is dumped.
