Step 2 · one house

How much compute can the heat needs of one house host?

The house needs heat every hour. Dividing that by the share of server heat that reaches the house gives the compute the house can host in that hour. The slider sets a constant compute demand; the results show how much of the heat requirement it covers and what the heat is worth over the whole year.
